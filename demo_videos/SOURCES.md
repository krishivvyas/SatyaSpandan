Notes
- The "fake" clips are fully generated people (Stable Diffusion frames / Veo), not face-swaps of a real person.
  SatyaSpandan's coherence check is designed for face-swaps; fully generated faces often carry no pulse at all
  and may come out "Unable to assess" rather than "Possible manipulation".
- The real clips show real, identifiable people. Use them for internal accuracy testing; for the live jury demo,
  prefer clips of consenting team members.
- Deepfake datasets with true face-swaps (FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, DFDC) and UBFC-rPPG need an access request.
